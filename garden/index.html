07

Build notes

read before you glue

One zone at a time

A single garden hose can't feed five zones at once without pressure crashing. Sequence them — the controller in B does it automatically; with A, stagger the timer start times.

Backflow first

The vacuum breaker on the inlet keeps fertilizer/soil from siphoning back into the house supply. Don't skip it — it's the cheapest line on the list.

Plastic, not brass

No freeze in San Diego, so ¾″ Sch-40 PVC everywhere. Brass valves are durability you'll never cash in here.

Unions = modular

Want it to break down like the photo? Drop a PVC union (≈$3) into each branch so a valve pops off without cutting pipe.
