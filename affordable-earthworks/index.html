I can help you do this.

When somebody googles excavation, grading, or demo in San Diego, Affordable Earthworks doesn’t come up — not on Maps, not anywhere. Three moves fix it, and I’ll do almost all of the work. Here’s the plan.

Move 1 Week one

Get on Google.

Google doesn’t have a listing for you — that’s the whole reason you’re not on Maps. I’ll set one up, claim your Yelp page (eight five-star reviews sitting on it already), and fix the wrong phone number a couple directories have. You don’t have to do anything for this one.

Move 2 Week two

Turn the website on.

One page at affordableearthworks.com, replacing the old site. Pick one of the three looks below and I’ll build it out. It’ll work on a phone, the number is tap-to-call, and quote requests go straight to you.

Move 3 Ongoing

Ask for reviews.

After a job, text the customer one link asking for a Google review. I’ll set up the link and write the text — you just copy it and send it. Reviews are what move you up the map, and this is the only part that’s yours to do.

“An artist with a backhoe.” one of the Yelp reviews — people already say this stuff about you
Your part Five things

What I need from you.

  • Pick a look from the three below.
  • The full-color logo, if you can find it.
  • A street address for Google’s verification postcard. It never gets published — the listing shows your service area, not your address.
  • Ten or so job photos off your phone. Before-and-afters are the good ones.
  • The GoDaddy login, so I can point the domain at the new page.
Proof Monthly

How you’ll know it’s working.

Google counts every call and every direction request your listing gets. Once a month I’ll send you the screenshot, and you can see for yourself.
