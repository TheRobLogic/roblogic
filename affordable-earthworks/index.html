The plan.

Google grading, excavation, or demolition in San Diego and Affordable Earthworks doesn’t come up. Three moves fix that in about thirty days. The website is the smallest of them, and nothing here costs anything to run.

MOVE 1 · WEEK ONE

Get on Google.

There is no Google listing for the business — that’s why it doesn’t show up on Maps. I create the listing, claim the Yelp page that already holds eight five-star reviews, and correct the wrong phone number a few directories carry. Nothing for you to do here.

MOVE 2 · WEEK TWO

Turn the website on.

One page at affordableearthworks.com, replacing the 2015 site. You pick one of the three looks below. It works on a phone, calls and quote requests come straight to you, and it’s free to host.

MOVE 3 · ONGOING

Ask for reviews.

After each job, text the customer one link asking for a Google review. I set up the link and write the message; you copy, paste, send. Reviews are what move a business up the map. This is the only recurring work, and it’s yours.

FROM YOU

What I need.

  • A pick from the three looks below.
  • The full-color logo, if it turns up.
  • A street address for Google’s mailed verification code. Never published — the listing shows a service area, not an address.
  • Ten job photos from your phone. Before-and-after pairs are best.
  • The GoDaddy login, so the domain can point at the new page.
PROOF

How you’ll know it’s working.

Google counts every call and direction request the listing gets; once a month you get the screenshot. If the numbers aren’t there, we stop. Nothing above has a monthly cost.
