The swap card

Paperclipped to the lid — every dairy swap that survived testing. The rule of the box: if a card needs cream, it earns a rewrite.

Dairy out, flavor in

Butter, for cooking→olive oil, or schmaltz for the brave
Butter, in baking→coconut oil, chilled solid, 1-for-1
Cream, in soups→blended white beans + a little broth
Milk, in pancakes→oat milk + 1 tsp vinegar, rest 5 min
Crema, on tacos→avocado whipped with lime + salt
Buttermilk, for slaw→tahini + lemon, or the mustard-vinegar dress

tested twice before it gets typed. house rule. —R
