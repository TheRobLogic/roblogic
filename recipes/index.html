Dairy-free swaps

Substitutions I use to keep things dairy-free.

Substitutions

Butter, for cooking→olive oil (or schmaltz)
Butter, in baking→coconut oil, chilled solid, 1-for-1
Cream, in soups→blended white beans + a little broth
Milk, in pancakes→oat milk + 1 tsp vinegar, rested 5 min
Crema, on tacos→avocado blended with lime + salt
Buttermilk, for slaw→tahini + lemon, or a mustard-vinegar dressing
