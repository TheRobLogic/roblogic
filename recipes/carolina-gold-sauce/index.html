Carolina Gold Mustard Sauce

Makes ~1½ cups · 10 min · dairy-free

Carolina Gold Mustard Sauce

Ingredients

  • ¾ cup yellow mustard
  • ¼ cup maple syrup (or honey)
  • 2 tbsp brown sugar
  • ¼ cup apple cider vinegar
  • 1 tbsp ketchup (optional, for body and color)
  • 1 tbsp tamari or vegan Worcestershire
  • 1 tsp hot sauce, or a pinch of cayenne
  • ½ tsp each black pepper and garlic powder

Method

  1. Whisk everything together in a small saucepan.
  2. Simmer on low for 5–8 minutes. Don't let it hard-boil; scorched mustard turns bitter.
  3. Taste and adjust.
  4. Let it cool. It keeps a couple of weeks in a jar in the fridge.

This is a South Carolina–style mustard barbecue sauce. Yellow mustard gives the tang and the color, maple and sugar give the sweetness, and vinegar keeps it bright. Don't swap Dijon in for all of the yellow mustard.

Adjusting the sweet and tang

  • For a rounder, sweeter sauce, add more maple and brown sugar.
  • For a sharper sauce, add more cider vinegar, and swap a tablespoon of the yellow mustard for whole-grain or Dijon.
  • Adjust one thing at a time and taste as you go.

Smoke

Smoke works well here. Use ½–1 tsp smoked paprika, a few drops of liquid smoke, or a spoonful of chipotle in adobo, which also adds heat and a little tomato.

Skip the coffee

Coffee adds a roasty bitterness that works in tomato-and-molasses sauces, but it muddies a bright, vinegar-forward mustard sauce like this one. For more depth here, use a little molasses, a splash of bourbon, or the tamari. Save the coffee for a tomato-based Kansas City sauce.

For the soy curls

Make it a little thicker than you would for ribs. The curls absorb some of it in the pot.
