Garden Kale Slaw

Serves 4–6 · 20 min + a rest · dairy-free

Garden Kale Slaw

Ingredients

  • 1 big bunch kale, de-stemmed and sliced thin
  • 2 carrots, grated or julienned
  • A few chard stems, sliced thin
  • ¼ onion, sliced thin
  • Handful of arugula, to finish

Dressing

  • 3 tbsp olive oil
  • 2 tbsp cider vinegar
  • 1 tbsp yellow mustard
  • 1–2 tsp maple, salt and pepper

Method

  1. De-stem and thin-slice the kale. Massage it for 2–3 minutes with a little oil and salt, until it darkens, softens, and shrinks by about half.
  2. Soak the sliced onion in cold water for 10 minutes (or quick-pickle it in the vinegar), then drain.
  3. Whisk the dressing. Toss with the kale, carrot, chard stems, and onion.
  4. Let it rest 30 minutes or more; it improves as it sits. Fold in the arugula just before serving.

You don't need cabbage. Its job in a slaw is to hold up to the dressing, and massaged kale does that just as well. It also keeps better — it improves as it sits, where cabbage slaw goes soggy. Here's what each vegetable does:

What each one does

  • Kale is the base, in place of cabbage. It's tough, so de-stem it, slice it thin, and massage it. That step matters most.
  • Carrot adds crunch and sweetness. Grate or julienne it.
  • Chard: use the stems, not the leaves. Sliced thin, the stems add crunch like celery. The leaves wilt, so save them for cooking.
  • Onion: mellow it first. A cold-water soak or a quick pickle takes the raw edge off.
  • Arugula is a finishing touch, not a base. It's peppery and wilts fast, so add it right before serving.

The dressing

The mustard-vinegar dressing uses the same base as the Carolina Gold sauce. It's good on its own and better piled on the BBQ soy curls in a sandwich, where the tang cuts the sweetness. Dairy-free.

Creamy version

For a creamy dressing, use tahini with lemon and garlic, or vegan mayo — both keep it dairy-free. Dress it at least 30 minutes ahead so the kale softens, and hold the arugula until serving.
