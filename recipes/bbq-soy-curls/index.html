BBQ Soy Curls

Serves 4 · rehydrate 10 min + slow-cook 1 hr · dairy-free

BBQ Soy Curls

Ingredients

  • 1 bag (8 oz) Butler Soy Curls
  • 2 cups broth, well seasoned
  • 1 tbsp oil, for browning
  • 1½ cups BBQ sauce (the Carolina Gold, or your own)
  • ½ tsp black pepper

Method

  1. Rehydrate the curls in warm seasoned broth for 10 minutes. Drain, then squeeze them dry.
  2. Brown them: dry-sauté in a hot pan, or roast or air-fry, until the edges crisp.
  3. Toss the browned curls with the BBQ sauce and pepper in the slow-cooker insert.
  4. Slow-cook on high for about 1 hour. (The Instant Pot slow-cook setting keeps them firmer.) Stir and serve.

Soy curls are whole soybeans, cooked and then dried, so there's no collagen to break down the way there is in meat. Long, slow cooking doesn't make them more tender — it just makes them mushy. They work like a sponge: once rehydrated and in sauce, they absorb most of their flavor early.

Don't cook them longer

Extra time in the cooker won't add flavor. It softens the curls and over-reduces a sugary sauce until it scorches at the edges. If you need to hold them, switch to the keep-warm setting instead of more high heat, and add a splash of broth if the sauce gets too thick.

Where the flavor comes from

  • Season the broth they rehydrate in — tamari, a few drops of liquid smoke, smoked salt, garlic and onion powder. The curls take that flavor in as they swell.
  • Squeeze them dry before saucing. Wet curls water down the sauce.
  • Brown them before saucing. The browned edges add more flavor than extra cooking time does.
  • Make the sauce a little thick. The curls absorb some of it in the pot.
