LOWER BODY

Goblet Squat

A beginner-friendly squat variation holding a dumbbell vertically at chest height. Trains quads, glutes, adductors, and trunk/upper-back as a postural anti-flexion brace.

Demo: Squat University

Form
  • Hold weight at sternum, elbows tucked down
  • Sit hips back and down between heels
  • Drive knees out, chest tall throughout
Avoid

Letting the chest collapse forward and lower back round at the bottom.

Mod

Squat to a box at a height that lets you keep a neutral spine; avoid deep flexion if anything tweaks.

Goblet Box Squat

Goblet squat performed to a box or bench, sitting briefly before standing back up. Reinforces depth control and posterior-chain loading through quads, glutes, and hamstrings.

Demo: Jason Brown

Form
  • Reach hips back to the box, not straight down
  • Pause seated, then drive feet through floor
  • Keep shins fairly vertical and chest proud
Avoid

Crashing onto the box and rocking backward instead of controlling the descent and pausing.

Mod

Higher box + lighter dumbbell keeps spine neutral and avoids any lumbar collapse onto the box.

Bulgarian Split Squat

Single-leg squat with the rear foot elevated on a bench, loaded with dumbbells at the sides. Hammers quads and glutes of the front leg while challenging hip stability and balance.

Demo: Athlean-X

Form
  • Long stance, rear foot laces-down on bench
  • Drop straight down, front shin tracks over toes
  • Drive through whole front foot to stand
Avoid

Stance too short so the front knee jams forward past the toes and the lower back arches.

Mod

Hold a wall or rail for balance and reduce range; keep torso slightly forward over front leg.

DB Romanian Deadlift

Hip-hinge pattern with dumbbells held in front of the thighs, lowered along the legs with soft knees. Primarily trains hamstrings and glutes with strong lat and erector engagement.

Demo: Athlean-X

Form
  • Push hips back, dumbbells slide down thighs
  • Long spine, ribs stacked over pelvis
  • Squeeze glutes to lock out hips at top
Avoid

Bending from the lower back instead of hinging at the hips, letting the spine round at the bottom.

Mod

Stop the dumbbells just below the kneecaps to keep a neutral spine; elevate dumbbells on plates if hamstrings are tight.

DB Sumo Deadlift

Wide-stance deadlift with toes turned out, dumbbell held vertically between the legs. Targets glutes, adductors, quads, and hamstrings while keeping the torso more upright than conventional.

Demo: Mike J2FIT

Form
  • Wide stance, toes 30–45 degrees out
  • Knees track over second and third toes
  • Push floor away — hips and chest rise together
Avoid

Letting the hips shoot up first so the lift turns into a stiff-legged deadlift with a rounded back.

Mod

Elevate the dumbbell on a low block to shorten range and keep spine neutral.

DB Hip Thrust

Glute-focused bridge with shoulders on a bench and a dumbbell across the hip crease. Loads hip extension, prioritizing glute max with hamstring assistance.

Demo: Jeff Nippard

Form
  • Shoulder blades on bench, chin tucked
  • Drive heels down, ribs stay stacked
  • Squeeze glutes to lock out, hips fully extend
Avoid

Hyperextending the lower back at the top instead of finishing with glute contraction.

Mod

Floor bridges with a folded towel under the dumbbell; cue posterior pelvic tilt to keep load off the lumbar spine.

DB Reverse Lunge

Single-leg lunge stepping backward with dumbbells at the sides. Trains quads, glutes, and hamstrings with less stress on the front knee than forward lunges.

Demo: Athlean-X

Form
  • Step back long, lower rear knee toward floor
  • Front shin near vertical, weight in front heel
  • Drive front foot to stand, return tall
Avoid

Step too short so the front knee shoots forward and the front foot lifts at the heel.

Mod

Reduce range or hold a single goblet to keep torso upright.

DB Step-up

Single-leg step onto a knee-height box or bench with dumbbells at the sides. Trains quads, glutes, and hamstrings of the working leg while challenging single-leg stability.

Demo: OPEX Fitness

Form
  • Whole foot on box, knee tracks over toes
  • Drive through working heel, no pushoff from rear
  • Stand tall, lower under control
Avoid

Pushing off the trailing foot to bounce up instead of driving through the working leg.

Mod

Lower box (mid-shin to just-below-knee) with lighter dumbbells; keep torso slightly forward over the working leg.

UPPER PUSH

DB Bench Press

Lying flat-bench press with a dumbbell in each hand, driving them up from chest level to lockout. Primary movers: pectoralis major, anterior deltoid, triceps.

Demo: Athlean-X

Form
  • Pinch shoulder blades back and down into bench
  • Slight arch, feet planted, drive through floor
  • Press in a slight arc, dumbbells over chest
Avoid

Flaring elbows to 90 degrees and bouncing dumbbells off the chest, which strains the shoulders.

Mod

Low incline (15–30°) and neutral (palms-facing) grip reduces shoulder strain.

DB Floor Press

Pressing dumbbells from the floor with the back of the upper arms used as a stop. Primary movers: pectoralis major (lockout portion), triceps, anterior deltoid.

Demo: Mike J2FIT

Form
  • Knees bent, dumbbells start above the chest
  • Lower until upper arms gently touch the floor
  • Pause briefly, then press straight back up
Avoid

Crashing the elbows into the floor instead of a controlled, paused tap.

Mod

Naturally shoulder-friendly. Cap reps in strongest range; keep elbows ~45° from torso.

DB Seated Shoulder Press

Seated overhead press with dumbbells, driving from shoulder height to lockout overhead. Primary movers: anterior and lateral deltoids, triceps, upper traps.

Demo: Athlean-X

Form
  • Sit tall against pad, ribs down, core braced
  • Press in scapular plane (slightly forward of frontal)
  • Stop just before elbows fully lock, control down
Avoid

Letting the lower back over-arch and the dumbbells drift behind the head.

Mod

Neutral (palms-facing) grip and stop at forehead height to spare the shoulders.

DB Push Press

Standing overhead press with a leg drive to launch the dumbbells past the sticking point. Primary movers: deltoids, triceps, plus quads, glutes, and core for the dip-drive.

Demo: CrossFit

Form
  • Shallow knee dip with vertical torso
  • Explode through the heels to drive dumbbells up
  • Finish with arms locked, ribs over hips
Avoid

Turning the dip into a quarter-squat or leaning back to muscle the weight overhead.

Mod

Sub the seated DB shoulder press if loaded spinal compression bothers you.

DB Tricep Overhead Extension

Two-handed dumbbell extension performed with the weight lowered behind the head, stretching the long head of the triceps under load. Primary mover: triceps brachii (especially long head).

Demo: Jeff Nippard

Form
  • Cup one dumbbell with both hands overhead
  • Keep elbows tucked and pointed forward
  • Lower behind head with control, then extend fully
Avoid

Flaring the elbows out so the load shifts off the triceps and onto the shoulders.

Mod

Sit on a bench with back support, run single-arm to spare lumbar and shoulders.

DB Chest Fly

Lying chest fly arcing the dumbbells out to the sides and back together, isolating the pecs through adduction. Primary mover: pectoralis major (sternal fibers).

Demo: Athlean-X

Form
  • Soft bend in the elbows, locked through the rep
  • Lower wide in an arc until pecs feel the stretch
  • Squeeze hands toward each other, not just up
Avoid

Bending the elbows like a press at the bottom — turns into a sloppy bench press.

Mod

Floor-fly variant caps range of motion and protects the anterior shoulder.

DB Lateral Raise

Standing or seated raise of dumbbells out to the sides until arms are roughly parallel to the floor. Primary mover: lateral (medial) deltoid.

Demo: Renaissance Periodization

Form
  • Slight forward lean, soft elbow bend held constant
  • Lead with the elbows, not the hands
  • Raise to about shoulder height, lower under control
Avoid

Swinging the torso and shrugging the traps to heave the dumbbells up.

Mod

Sit on an incline bench or against a wall to remove momentum.

UPPER PULL

One-arm DB Row

Single-arm dumbbell row performed with the off-hand braced on a bench so the torso stays supported and the working side can pull through a full range. Primary muscles: lats, mid-traps, rhomboids, rear delts, biceps.

Demo: Athlean-X

Form
  • Brace torso parallel to floor, lock spine neutral
  • Drive elbow back toward the hip, not straight up
  • Pause at top with shoulder blade pulled toward spine
Avoid

Yanking the dumbbell with body twist — sheds load to lower back and erases lat tension.

Mod

Chest-supported variation on an incline bench takes the lumbar spine out of it entirely.

DB Renegade Row

A plank position held on two dumbbells while alternating one-arm rows, blending an anti-rotation core challenge with a pulling pattern. Primary muscles: lats, rhomboids, rear delts, obliques, deep core.

Demo: Mind Pump TV

Form
  • Set feet wide, squeeze glutes to lock the plank
  • Row elbow tight to ribs without rotating hips
  • Keep both dumbbells stacked under the shoulders
Avoid

Letting the hips twist toward the ceiling on each rep — kills the anti-rotation purpose.

Mod

Regress to chest-supported row on an incline bench if shoulder/wrist sensitive.

DB Pullover

Lying across or along a bench, lower a single dumbbell behind the head with a slight elbow bend, then pull it back over the chest. Primary muscles: lats, long head of triceps, serratus, pecs.

Demo: Athlean-X

Form
  • Soft, fixed elbow bend — no flexion or extension at elbow
  • Drive ribcage down so the lats own the stretch
  • Stop the pull when dumbbell is over the upper chest
Avoid

Bending and straightening the elbows turns it into a triceps extension.

Mod

Shorten the bottom range so the dumbbell doesn't pass the plane of the head if shoulder is cranky.

DB Curl

Standing dumbbell curl with both arms simultaneously and a supinated grip. Primary muscles: biceps brachii (long and short heads), brachialis, brachioradialis.

Demo: Athlean-X

Form
  • Pin elbows at ribs, don't let them drift forward
  • Supinate as dumbbell rises — palms face shoulder at top
  • Lower under control until arm is fully extended
Avoid

Swinging at the hips and using momentum to lift heavier weight.

Mod

Seated on a bench with a back pad locks out body english.

DB Hammer Curl

Curl variation performed with a neutral grip (palms facing each other) so the wrists never supinate. Primary muscles: brachialis, brachioradialis, biceps long head.

Demo: Mind Pump TV

Form
  • True neutral grip throughout, no rotation
  • Elbows pinned, curl straight up toward shoulder
  • 2–3 second eccentric on the way down
Avoid

Letting elbows flare forward at the top — converts to a partial front raise.

Mod

Cross-body hammer curl tracking toward the opposite shoulder is gentler on the joint.

Banded Pull-apart

Standing with arms extended forward, pull a light resistance band apart by retracting the shoulder blades. Primary muscles: rhomboids, mid and lower traps, rear delts, external rotators.

Demo: Athlean-X

Form
  • Start with arms straight at chest height, thumbs up
  • Pull from shoulder blades, not hands
  • Ribs stacked over hips, no lower-back arch
Avoid

Bending elbows to compensate when the band is too heavy — converts to a row.

Mod

Lighter band, palms supinated to bias external rotation if shoulder is cranky.

Band Face-pull

Pull a band anchored at face height back toward the forehead while flaring the elbows up and rotating the shoulders externally. Primary muscles: rear delts, mid and lower traps, rotator cuff.

Demo: Athlean-X

Form
  • Anchor at or just above forehead height
  • Lead with the elbows up and out, not down
  • Finish with hands beside temples, knuckles back
Avoid

Pulling toward the chin with elbows dropped — turns it into a row.

Mod

Lower the anchor to chest height and reduce range, focus on external rotation only.

CORE & MOBILITY

Bird-dog

Quadruped anti-rotation core stability drill — extend opposite arm and leg while holding the trunk still. Trains spinal stiffness and cross-body coordination without loading the spine.

Demo: Squat University

Form
  • Make a flat tabletop — hips and shoulders square
  • Reach arm and opposite leg long, not high
  • Brace abs as if bracing for a punch the entire rep
Avoid

Letting the hips rotate or the low back sag when the leg lifts.

Mod

Lift just the arm or just the leg first, holding 5–10 seconds, before adding both.

Modified Curl-up (McGill)

Dr. Stuart McGill's spine-sparing abdominal exercise: hands wedged under the lumbar spine, one knee bent, head and shoulders lifted as one rigid unit. Builds anterior core endurance without flexing the lumbar spine.

Demo: Brian Carroll (with Stuart McGill)

Form
  • Hands palm-down under small of low back to preserve curve
  • Lift head, shoulders, and upper chest as one block
  • Hold 7–10 seconds with steady breathing
Avoid

Crunching the lumbar spine into the floor — kills the spine-sparing intent.

Mod

3–5 second holds and lift only the head if shoulders cranky.

Side Plank

A lateral isometric loading the obliques, quadratus lumborum, and glute medius while keeping the spine neutral. Key for frontal-plane core stability and hip stability.

Demo: Squat University

Form
  • Stack shoulder directly over the support elbow
  • Drive hips up — straight line head to feet
  • Squeeze the down-side glute and brace obliques
Avoid

Letting hips sag toward the floor or rotating chest down.

Mod

From-knees side plank — bend knees 90° and pivot off the bottom knee.

Dead Bug

Supine anti-extension core exercise — opposite arm and leg lower while the low back stays pinned to the floor. Trains lumbar stability with limb movement.

Demo: Mike Robertson

Form
  • Press low back firmly into floor before any limb moves
  • Exhale fully as arm and opposite leg extend
  • Move slow, only as low as you keep back flat
Avoid

Letting the lower back arch off the floor as the leg extends.

Mod

Tabletop dead bug — keep knees bent at 90°, only lower one heel to tap the floor.

Pallof Press

Standing anti-rotation press — holding a band at chest level, press the handle straight out while resisting being twisted toward the anchor. Trains rotary core stability.

Demo: Mind Pump TV

Form
  • Stand perpendicular to anchor, ribs stacked over hips
  • Press hands out from sternum without trunk rotation
  • Pause 2 seconds at extension before pulling slowly back
Avoid

Allowing shoulders or hips to twist toward the anchor instead of staying square.

Mod

Half-kneeling Pallof press with the inside knee down to remove balance demand.

Suitcase Carry

Loaded one-arm walking carry with a single dumbbell or kettlebell at the side. Trains lateral core stability, grip, and posture under asymmetrical load.

Demo: Squat University

Form
  • Stand tall, ribs down, shoulders packed before step one
  • Don't let loaded shoulder dip — stay square and even
  • Short controlled steps, breathe through a braced trunk
Avoid

Side-bending toward the weight or hiking the loaded shoulder up to the ear.

Mod

Suitcase HOLD — stand still in posture for 20–30s before adding walking.

Cat-cow

Quadruped spinal mobility drill that gently moves the spine through flexion (cat) and extension (cow). Lubricates spinal segments and reduces morning stiffness.

Demo: Physiofitness PT

Form
  • Hands under shoulders, knees under hips — neutral start
  • Inhale into cow: tailbone up, chest forward, gentle gaze ahead
  • Exhale into cat: tuck tailbone, round mid-back, chin to chest
Avoid

Forcing end-range arching or rounding instead of moving smoothly.

Mod

Reduce range to small pain-free oscillations if either direction tweaks.

90/90 Hip Stretch

Seated hip mobility position with the front leg in 90° external rotation and the back leg in 90° internal rotation. Opens the hip capsule in both rotational directions.

Demo: Tom Morrison

Form
  • Both knees bent to 90 — front shin parallel to chest
  • Sit tall, chest up, no low-back round
  • Lean forward only as far as front sit-bone stays grounded
Avoid

Letting the front-side hip lift off the floor to chase more forward lean.

Mod

Sit on a yoga block or folded towel under front-side glute to reduce required hip rotation.
