Your inbox assistant

The other thing you asked about — help with the inbox. Here’s what I’ll set it up to do. Think of it as a tireless front-desk helper: it reads every email the moment it lands and gets it ready for you, so the routine stuff is handled fast and nothing ever slips through the cracks.

Reads every email — day or night

The moment something comes in — 2pm or 2am, weekday or weekend — it’s read and sorted. That after-hours inquiry that used to sit unanswered until morning gets caught right away.

Writes the reply for you

For the common questions — what you do, how the cross-border visit works, your service area, what to expect — it drafts a warm, on-brand answer and pings you. You read it and hit send. It handles the routine, you stay in every real conversation.

A short brief every morning

One quick, plain-English rundown in your inbox: what came in, who’s waiting on a reply, and which conversations have gone quiet and need a nudge. No digging through threads to see where things stand.

Nothing slips — every lead tracked

The part you said matters most: every inquiry is followed from first hello to booked trip, in a running leads-and-follow-ups list — so the people who need a follow-up actually get one.

Built to your one rule. It answers common questions, but it never quotes a firm price and never confirms availability — those always come from you. It drafts and organizes; you approve and send. And it plugs straight into the Gmail you already use, so there’s nothing new to learn.
