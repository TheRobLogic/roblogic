How a PetMex day goes.

You text us. We do everything else — and you get updates the entire way.

1

We come to your door

Pickup at home anywhere in our San Diego service area. Your pet rides in the car with us — never kenneled.

2

Across the border, we drive

We handle all the documentation and the crossing. It's common and completely legal — and you don't lift a finger.

3

Trusted vet, we translate

We stay beside your pet at clinics we personally trust, send real-time updates, and translate every word the vet says.

4

Home, same trip

Back to your door the same day for most visits — with a happier, healthier pet and a much smaller bill.
