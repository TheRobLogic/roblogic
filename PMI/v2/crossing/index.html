The Premise

Quality veterinary care shouldn't be rationed by a price tag — or by the dread of the drive that gets you there.

PetMex is a family-owned, San Diego–based service with a simple idea: take the headache out of the trip to the vet. We pick your pet up at your door, drive them across the border to a clinic we personally trust in Tijuana, stay beside them through the whole visit translating every word, and bring them home — all in the same trip. Owners often save anywhere from 10 to 90% versus a U.S. bill, without trading away the care.

What follows is that journey, told the way we live it — in four chapters, from the pickup to the welcome home.

Chapter Two · The Border

The Crossing

MX

Pictured — The quiet stretch. Pets settle; we keep talking to them the whole way.

Frontera Tijuana Documented · Legal

Crossing the border with a pet is common and entirely legal — and we do the documentation so you never have to.

For safety and border-crossing reasons, owners can't ride along. So the moment your pet leaves your arms, they're in ours. We stay with them the entire time, send you real-time updates from the road and the clinic, and translate everything the vet says — because being bilingual isn't a feature here, it's the whole point. Hablamos Español.

If anything ever comes up, you don't hear about it after the fact. We call you immediately, so you and the vet decide together.

Chapter Three · The Care

The Clinic

Dra.

Pictured — Examination room, Tijuana. The vets we work with we personally trust.

Examined Cleared Specialist care

This is the chapter where a routine visit quietly becomes the reason it mattered.

Take Louie. He came down for what his family thought was an ordinary check-up. It was Dra Martha — one of the specialists we work with — who caught his thyroid condition. As his owner Rebeca put it, Dra Martha "literally found out about our Louie's thyroid." That's the difference between a vet who clears the appointment and one who looks closer.

We partner only with qualified, experienced veterinarians and specialists — for surgery, dentistry, imaging, neurology, ophthalmology, and more. Throughout the visit we stay in the room, relay every finding back to you in English, and never make a care decision on your behalf.
