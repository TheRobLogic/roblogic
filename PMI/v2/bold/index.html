Good questions

You ask.
We answer.

The stuff every pet owner wants to know before they hand us the leash. Don't see yours? Call or text and we'll walk you through it.

¿Hablan español? ¡Sí! Somos completamente bilingües.
Are you a clinic?

No — we're the transport. We take your pet to trusted vet clinics in Tijuana and back, door to door. The veterinary work is done by the qualified clinics and specialists we partner with.

Is it legal? Do I need documents?

Yes — it's common and completely legal. We handle all the border-crossing documentation for you, so there's nothing for you to chase down.

Are the Tijuana vets qualified?

Absolutely. We work only with qualified, experienced veterinarians and specialists we personally trust — the same clinics we'd use for our own pets.

Can I come along for the ride?

No — for safety and border-crossing reasons we aren't able to take owners along. But we stay with your pet the whole time, send real-time updates, and translate everything the vet says.

What if something goes wrong?

We're with your pet the entire time and call you immediately so you can decide together with the vet. You're always part of the decision.

How long does it take?

It depends on the visit — many are same-day. We give you a time estimate up front so you know what to expect.
