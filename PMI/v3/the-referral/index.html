How a referral works

Five quiet steps, start to home.

  1. You tell us what's going on

    Fill out a short intake. What your pet needs, what's been worrying you, where you are. No phone tag required.

  2. We match the clinic & confirm the name

    We pair your pet with the right partner clinic and specialist, then confirm the clinic, the attending veterinarian, and the plan with you in writing.

  3. We pick up — VIP, door to door

    Your pet rides in comfort across the border in our care — a calm, comfortable ride straight to the exam room.

  4. The veterinarian provides care

    You get the diagnosis, the plan, and the pricing from the clinic. We keep you in the loop the whole visit.

  5. We bring them safely home

    Back across the border and back to your door — usually the same day. That's the whole headache, handled.

Honest answers

The questions everyone asks.

Are you a clinic?

No — and that's the point. We're the transport and the referral. We don't perform the care; we connect you with the partner veterinary clinics in Tijuana that do, and we get your pet there and back safely. Think of us as the bridge between you and a vet you can trust.

Are you going to steal my dog?

We get this one a lot, and we love that you asked — it means you care. Absolutely not. We're a licensed, family-run service with a real name and a real reputation. Your pet is in our care for one round trip to the vet and right back to your door. You'll know the clinic, the veterinarian, and the timeline before we ever pick up.

I can't speak Spanish — is that a problem?

Not at all. We handle everything across the border for you, in both languages. Hablamos español. You communicate with us in English; we take care of the rest with the clinic. You never have to navigate a thing in another language.

¿Ya usé una clínica en Tijuana antes?

¡Qué bueno! Si ya tiene una clínica de confianza en Tijuana, con gusto coordinamos el transporte de ida y vuelta para que su mascota llegue cómoda y segura. If you've already used a clinic in Tijuana before: wonderful. We'll happily coordinate the round-trip transport to your trusted clinic — or help you find the right specialist among our partners. You choose; we handle the road.

What's your service area?

We serve Lakeside, Santee, and El Cajon — the communities south of the 52. Our hours are Monday through Friday, 8 AM to 4 PM. Weekend transport is available for an additional fee.

Do you handle emergencies?

No. We do not offer emergency services. PetMex is a scheduled, by-appointment transport service — we are not an emergency or 24/7 provider. If your pet has a medical emergency, please contact a local emergency veterinary hospital right away.
