The short version

A family business that drives your pet to better, kinder, more affordable care.

PetMex International is a small, family-run service started by TJ and Lizette. We pick your pet up on the San Diego side, drive them in comfort across the border to a vetted partner clinic in Tijuana, stay close through their visit, and bring them safely home to you.

The idea is simple. Quality veterinary care in Tijuana costs a fraction of what the same procedure runs at home — without cutting the corners that matter. The part most people dread is the logistics: the drive, the border, the language, the parking, the unknown. So we handle all of it. You hand us a pet and a worry; we hand you back a healthy pet and a much smaller bill.

“We take the headache out of that trip to the vet.” The whole job, in one sentence

Think of it as door-to-door care. Same-day round trip, with people who treat your animal the way they treat their own. We are bilingual, we know the clinics personally, and we have made this drive more times than we can count — so you only have to do it once: today, with us.

Good questions

The things people actually ask us.

Are you a clinic?

No — we’re the transport, not the vet. PetMex is a VIP pet transportation service. We drive your pet to trusted, full-service partner clinics in Tijuana where the actual veterinary care happens, and we bring them home. Think of us as the ride, and the clinic as the destination.

Are you going to steal my dog?

No — and we love that you asked. Trusting a stranger with your animal is a big deal, so we keep everything transparent: you know exactly which clinic we’re going to, it’s a same-day round trip, and you get your pet back the same day. We’re a family business with a name and a reputation in this community, not a faceless service.

Can’t speak Spanish? Sin problema

No problem at all. We’re fully bilingual and handle every conversation at the clinic and the border for you. You never have to translate a thing — that’s part of what you’re paying us to take off your plate.

I’ve used a clinic in Tijuana before.

Great — then you already know the savings are real. If you have a clinic you trust, tell us; in many cases we can take you right to it. If you’d rather we recommend one, we work with partners we know personally and stand behind. Either way, we handle the driving, the border, and the logistics.

¿Hablan Español? En español

Sí, claro. Somos un negocio familiar y hablamos español con gusto. Nos encargamos de todo — el viaje, la frontera y la comunicación con la clínica — para que usted no tenga que preocuparse por nada.

(Yes, of course. We’re a family business and we’re glad to help you in Spanish — the drive, the border, and the clinic, all handled.)

What about emergencies?

We do not offer emergency services. PetMex is a scheduled, by-appointment transport service operating Monday through Friday, 8 AM–4 PM — not a 24/7 or emergency provider. If your pet has a medical emergency, please contact a local emergency veterinary hospital right away.
