07Straight answers

The questions everyone actually has.

These are real questions we get, answered in our own words. No dodging.

Are you going to steal my dog?

No. We understand exactly why you'd ask — handing your pet to someone who drives them across a border is a real leap of trust. So here's the honest answer: we're a licensed, family-run business with our names and faces on this page. We bring your pet to a scheduled clinic appointment and return them to your door the same day. You'll know the clinic, the date, and the plan before we ever pick up your pet.

Are you a clinic?

No — and we won't pretend to be. PetMex is a transportation service. We don't practice veterinary medicine. We get your pet safely to a trusted, established partner clinic in Tijuana, stay on top of the visit, and bring them home. The medical care is done by licensed vets at the clinic.

Can't speak Spanish?

That's completely fine — you don't need a word of it. We handle all the communication with the clinic for you, in both languages. Hablamos Español. Your only job is to tell us, in English, what your pet needs.

I've used a clinic in Tijuana before.

Great — then you already know the savings are real. Where we come in is the part people dread: the driving, the border, the parking, the language, the waiting. We take the headache out of that trip to the vet so you get the same affordable care without giving up your whole day or your nerves.

Do you handle emergencies?

No. We do NOT offer emergency services. We are a scheduled, appointment-based transport service operating Monday–Friday, 8–4. If your pet has an emergency, please contact your local emergency veterinarian right away. See the notice below for the plain version.
