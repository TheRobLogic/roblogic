Plain answers

Questions, answered straight.

The things owners actually ask us — kept in our own words, because the honest answer is usually the short one.

Are you a clinic? +
No. PetMex International is a VIP pet transportation service. We don't perform veterinary care ourselves — we drive your pet to and from trusted, licensed partner clinics in Tijuana, where their own veterinarians do the work.
Are you going to steal my dog? +
Never. We're a family business and your pet is treated like our own — picked up at your door, handed to the partner clinic, and brought right back home to you. The whole visit is documented and transparent, start to finish. That's the entire point of showing you the chart.
Can't speak Spanish? +
No problem at all. Hablamos español. We handle all the communication with the partner clinic on your behalf, in either language, so nothing gets lost in translation.
I've used a clinic in Tijuana before +
Great — then you already know the savings are real. We take the headache out of that trip: the border, the drive, the logistics. You stay home; your pet still gets the care and the price you crossed the border for.
¿Hablan español? ES+
Sí, hablamos español. Somos un negocio familiar y atendemos a su mascota como si fuera nuestra: la recogemos en su puerta, la llevamos a una clínica asociada de confianza en Tijuana y la regresamos a casa con usted. Todo el costo se le explica por adelantado — sin sorpresas.
What does the price include? +
The tiers on this page cover your pet's round-trip transport, starting at $200 / $250 / $300 depending on the visit, plus $150 for a weekend window. The veterinary procedure is quoted separately and honestly by the partner clinic. Final price depends on destination and needs.
Where do you pick up, and when? +
We serve Lakeside, Santee and El Cajon — south of the 52. Our hours are Monday–Friday, 8 to 4, with optional weekend service for an added fee.
