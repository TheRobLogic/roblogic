We’ve Heard Them All

Yes, absolutely. Thousands of San Diego pet owners take their pets to Tijuana for veterinary care every year — in 2022 alone, over 33,500 pets crossed the border from the US to Mexico. We handle all of the documentation and border-crossing logistics for you, so it’s smooth and worry-free.
We work only with qualified, experienced veterinarians and specialists we personally trust — the same clinics we’d use for our own pets. We’ve gotten to know each one, and your pet only ever goes to a clinic we’d feel completely comfortable using ourselves.
We stay with your pet the entire time. We send real-time updates and translate every finding from the vet directly to you. If anything comes up, we call you right away so you can make decisions together with the doctor — you’re always in the loop.
Our service is fully door-to-door so you don’t have to. For safety and border-crossing reasons, we aren’t able to take owners along — but here’s our promise: we stay with your pet the entire time, send real-time photo and text updates, and translate everything the vet says directly to you. You’ll feel right there with them, every step of the way.
It depends on the procedure. A simple consultation or vaccine visit can be done in a few hours. Dental cleanings and minor surgeries are typically same-day. Bigger surgeries may need an overnight stay. We always give you a time estimate before the trip so you know exactly what to expect.
We primarily transport dogs and cats, but we’ve cared for other companion animals too. Every pet rides comfortably with us — never kenneled, never crowded. Give us a call and we’ll happily talk through your pet’s needs.
You don’t need a thing — we handle it all. PetMex takes care of every piece of border-crossing documentation and veterinary paperwork, so your pet’s trip is smooth, organized, and fully legal.
