Methodology. Three research agents were dispatched in parallel on April 18, 2026: (1) Democratic candidate funding, (2) Republican + minor-party funding, (3) scandals and conspiracies across all candidates. Their outputs were verified, cross-referenced, and synthesized into these dossiers.

Labeling. All factual claims carry source links. Items marked "Allegation" are disputed accusations under active reporting. Items marked "Theory" are circulating conspiracies flagged as such — not endorsed as fact.

Caveats. Campaign finance figures reflect filings through Dec 31, 2025 unless noted. Q1 2026 semi-annual reports will reshuffle. Independent expenditure disclosures lag and typically spike closer to the primary. CalAccess (CA Sec of State) is the authoritative primary source.
What's next. Emerson polling continues every ~3 weeks through the primary. The June 2 top-two election will produce two finalists for the November general. Given the current fragmentation of the Democratic field and the coordinated Republican top-two lockout attempt, a Hilton-vs-Bianco November remains the modal scenario.
