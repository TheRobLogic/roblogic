1

The establishment is sitting out

Newsom, Pelosi, and Harris have all declined to endorse any Democrat in the primary. No consensus establishment candidate has emerged. (Newsom told WaPo on Apr 28 he is "supporting a Democrat in the runoff.")

2

Two billionaire money models

Steyer self-funds (~$147M per the CalMatters May filings tracker — 2nd-priciest CA gov race ever behind Whitman 2010 $176M). Mahan is bankrolled by a Silicon Valley billionaire coalition (~$8.6M from ~25 tech donors, plus ~$25M+ across two tech-sector IE PACs; "California Back to Basics" alone exceeds $15M per CNN/AP/Fortune May 7).

New (Apr 23–27): Anti-Steyer ad stack scaled to ~$14M — PG&E ($10M routed via shell), CA Realtors ($5M), CA BIA ($1M), CCPOA, IBEW 1245.

3

The GOP is split in two

Hilton = tech-right + Murdoch + Trump endorsement. Bianco = cops + MAGA + stop-the-steal. CAGOP convention refused to pick either.

4

Live scandal on the Republican side

AG Bonta has an active civil-rights investigation of Bianco's sheriff's department. CA Supreme Court halted the department's 650K-ballot seizure and granted Bonta's request for an expedited briefing schedule.

The removal

UC Santa Barbara associate professor (History, Black Studies, Islamic Studies). Previously Jill Stein's 2024 Green Party VP running mate.

Timeline of disqualification. The California Secretary of State removed Ware from the 2026 gubernatorial ballot in March 2026 over filing deficiencies described as "mismatched returns and inappropriately redacted names" in his candidate filings. On April 1, 2026, Sacramento Superior Court Judge James Arguelles denied his state-court lawsuit to be reinstated. On April 17, 2026, the California Court of Appeal summarily refused to reinstate him. On May 11, 2026, U.S. District Judge William B. Shubb (E.D. Cal., 2:26-cv-1643) declined to reinstate Ware on the June 2 ballot, ruling on timing grounds — "the primary election is already underway. Already several hundred thousand Californians have voted" — and did not reach the constitutional challenge to AB 880's tax-return-disclosure law, which is deferred for later disposition. Ware indicates appeal. The Green Party has no candidate on the June 2 gubernatorial ballot.

On April 23, 2026, Current Affairs magazine published a long-form investigation alleging procedural irregularities in how the Secretary of State handled the disqualification.
