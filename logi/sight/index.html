Diagram 1 — Room

where the two cables go

The Sight sits mid-table and needs exactly two cables: a short power jumper to the PoE+ injector at the table, and one data run across the room to a small host dongle that plugs straight into the Rally Bar. The Sight never touches the Tap, the network, or yesterday's TV chain.

If the run uses the A07 wall plate, the whole path plate-to-bar must still be shielded Cat6A with no switch in between — it carries USB, not Ethernet.

Diagram 2 — Connections

every jack · every rule

The Sight base has three network-style jacks, but none of them are real network ports: POWER just takes power from the injector, DATA is USB dressed up in a network cable (runs to the little adapter at the bar), and MIC POD is only for optional extra mics. Power and data never share a cable.

The Hookup

in order · no pairing step exists
  1. Power first. Wall outlet → the black Phihong power injector → Sight POWER jack (short cable at the table). The injector's own light tells you how it went: steady green = it's powering the Sight · slow amber blink = powered up but doesn't feel the Sight on the line (problem's at the far end) · fast amber blink = something's shorted, unplug. Good news from the parts pile: there are two injectors on site — the white wall-plug one (label: "IEEE802.3at compliant PSE") is the Tap kit's and stays with the Tap; the black Phihong is free, and it's the Sight's. One injector can never feed two things, but you don't need it to.
  2. The run. Sight DATA jack → shielded cable → the little adapter at the bar. The kit does NOT include this cable — you bring it, and it has to be the heavy shielded kind: look for "S/FTP" and "Cat6A" printed on the jacket, and a shiny metal tip on the plug. All-plastic plug = ordinary network cable = will not work (the Cat5e cable from the Tap's box is exactly that — don't use it). The path must be one straight line to the bar, never through a network switch. Want to use the A07 wall plate? It's the low plate on the wall behind the Sight's corner — bottom jack labeled A07, black cable already in it (7/21 photo below). Plug a laptop into it first: if the laptop gets internet or a link light, that jack is live network — don't use it. Totally dead = perfect.
  3. The adapter ("host dongle"). Small box from the kit: network jack on one side, two short tails on the other. The run plugs into its jack; its two tails plug straight into the back of the bar — one into any USB-A port (all 3 are fine), one into the MIC POD port. Straight in, never through a USB hub.
  4. Watch it boot. LED loops blue → lilac → white → cyan, then solid cyan = ready. The real go/no-go: it appears under Settings › Peripherals on the bar. Cyan but not listed = see the rescue list at the bottom — on 2.0.x firmware that's a known bug, not your wiring.
  5. Turn it on as a camera. Settings › Camera › RightSight 2 › Speaker View. The automatic camera hand-off needs the bar's software (CollabOS) at 1.13 or newer — it's on by itself from there, nothing extra to flip. Newest is 2.1.0 and it fixes exactly the "Sight doesn't show up" bugs, so updating is worth it. Updating happens right on the bar: Settings › Updates › Update, about 20 minutes, no Teams admin needed.
  6. Admin PIN reality. Teams mode has no factory default PIN — if none was ever set, it prompts you to create one on the spot. If a PIN bounces you, one was set by the installer or pushed from Teams admin (1234 is only the non-Teams default). Reset = Teams admin only: Devices › this room › configuration profile with "Clear admin default password" — then the Tap lets you create a fresh one. Locked out? You may not need Settings at all: hand-off is on by default since 2.0.A — jump straight to the test call, and check the in-call camera icon for the Sight. Alone in the room, its LED sitting orange = "inactive, one person" is normal, not a fault.
  7. Prove it. Live test call — talk from different seats, watch the camera hand off between bar and Sight. Then log the serial off the base label.
From the base label: Sight model VU0067 · powered over the cable, needs the 30W class (label says "802.3at" or "PoE+") — the common 15W "af" kind won't wake it.
Verify onsite: A07 run shielded + not live network (laptop test) · what the Phihong feeds today · bar software 1.13+ · a real shielded Cat6A cable on the truck — the kit doesn't include one.
Not involved: the Tap · the LAN · yesterday's TV/HDMI chain.

Your Parts, By Sight

ID'd from the 2026-07-22 photos
  1. Black tower, "logi" on the front = the Sight. The lens still has its clear shipping film — peel it before the test call or the picture will be soft.
  2. Black stick that says "SIGHT Dongle" on the back (Model VU0068) = the adapter for the bar. Its cable splits into two tails: a normal USB-A plug and a smaller one — USB-A tail into any USB-A port on the bar, small tail into the MIC POD port.
  3. WHITE stick that says "Dongle Transceiver" = the Tap's, not yours today. Easy rule: black stick = Sight, white stick = Tap.
  4. White wall plug, label "Single Port Power over Ethernet · IEEE802.3at" = the Tap's power. It stays with the Tap.
  5. Black Phihong brick = the Sight's power. A black power-brick the size of a big laptop charger — "PHIHONG" and "MODEL: POE90U-1BT" on the label, AC cord in one end, two network jacks on the other. Last seen lying by the equipment-leg / floor-box area in the 7/21 photos. Its output goes to the Sight's POWER jack (the one ringed in yellow on the base).
  6. On the Sight's base: POWER = yellow-ringed jack · DATA = the jack with the little two-tailed-adapter drawing etched next to it · the third jack (in the black rubber cutout) = mic pods / second Sight, unused today.
  7. Long grey cable coiled around the Sight's base, shiny metal plug tips = your data-run candidate. One check left: read the printing on the jacket. Says CAT6A (with S/FTP or SSTP) → that's the run, use it. Says CAT5E → it's a Tap-kit spare, don't use it.
  8. Back of the bar, from the photos: HDMI-TV1 and Ethernet are plugged (correct, yesterday's fix), HDMI-TV2 empty (correct — leave it), USB-A ports and MIC POD port free and waiting for the dongle.

If It Won't Show Up

rescue list · most likely first
  1. Cyan but not in Peripherals? Firmware first, cables second — every 2.0.x build has a documented bug matching this exact symptom, fixed in 2.1.0 (Settings › Updates). Already on 2.1+? Reseat the DATA cable at both ends and the dongle's two leads → power-cycle the Sight (pull the injector-side plug 30 s — it has no power button) → reboot the bar → try one of the other USB-A ports (any of the 3 is valid).
  2. LED loops forever, never settles cyan? Confirm the injector shows solid green, power-cycle once, then factory reset (below). Still looping = dead unit — 2-year warranty, stop burning time on it.
  3. No light at all, but the injector shows green? The cable path is the suspect: not truly shielded the whole way, longer than 20 m, or a bad jack. Swap in a known-good shielded cable before blaming the Sight.
  4. Lilac pulse? It's updating — hands off. No documented duration; only if it's clearly stuck well past ~15 min, power-cycle and let it retry.
  5. Sight factory reset: hold Reset + one mic-mute button for 10 s (no pinhole, no beep). It then sits dark for up to 5 minutes before rebooting — that's normal, don't panic-unplug.

Hard datasheet limit: 20 m dongle-to-Sight, no exceptions — if you see a 40 m figure anywhere, it's wrong.
