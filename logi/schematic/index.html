Diagram 1 — Room

physical routing

Two 86″ Philips displays face each other across the board table. The Kramer switch + ClearOne DSP live inside a table leg; cabling drops into the floor box, runs the aluminum raceway, then climbs each wall into the ceiling and back down to each TV. The Rally Bar hangs under the near-wall TV; the far TV is bare, fed by a regular HDMI run up the wall and across the ceiling — no Cat extender in this room.

Routing simplified to bundles — exact per-wire logic is Diagram 2.

Diagram 2 — Signal Path

wiring · the fix

Teams appliance mode can't mirror the Rally Bar's two HDMI outputs (extend-only). So the meeting rides one output — HDMI-TV1 → Kramer IN2 — and the room's existing one-source-to-two-TV distribution paints both walls. A laptop on IN1 wins by priority and takes both screens; unplug it and the meeting returns.

The Fix

no new parts while the old distribution works
  1. Rally Bar HDMI-TV1 → Kramer IN 2. The only new cable. Leave the bar's HDMI-TV2 empty so it outputs a single mirrored picture. Bring 6 / 25 / 50 ft.
  2. Laptop plate stays on Kramer IN 1. Priority is fixed IN1 > IN2 — plug a laptop in and it takes both screens; unplug and the meeting returns automatically.
  3. Pull the green Phoenix plug off the Kramer. Otherwise meeting audio doubles through the ClearOne ceiling speakers against the bar = echo.
