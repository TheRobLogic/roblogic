The Job

read once

A Logitech Rally Bar in Teams appliance mode shows the meeting on the one TV it's plugged into. The room has two TVs on opposite walls, and everyone should see the same picture on both.

Teams mode cannot mirror two screens in software — verified in Microsoft's and Logitech's own docs. Its dual-display feature is extend-only (gallery on one, content on the other) and needs a Pro license. The fix is plumbing, not settings: feed the bar's single output through the room's existing one-source-to-two-TV distribution — the same path the old laptop input used. The bar outputs 1080p max, which the old path already handles.

VERDICT: re-point one HDMI cable, pull one audio plug, set two TV menus, test. $0 in parts while the old plumbing works.

FAQ

6 entries
  • Why won't the Rally Bar just do this itself?
    Teams appliance mode has no mirror/duplicate option — confirmed in Microsoft's release notes and Logitech's CollabOS docs through mid-2026. Ironically, BYOD mode (bar as a USB webcam for a laptop) mirrors both ports natively. Teams mode is the one that can't.
  • So was the hardware broken this whole time?
    No. Nothing was missing or defective. The one-display behavior is a Teams software limitation. The "dual display" the product sheet mentions is real, but it means extend (gallery + content split) — and it needs a Teams Rooms Pro license to even turn on.
  • Will plugging a laptop in at the desk still work?
    Better than before — plug in and the laptop takes both screens; unplug and the meeting view comes back. That's the Kramer's Priority auto-switch doing its factory-default job.
  • What about the touchscreen and the table camera cables?
    The Cat5 lines from the table are the Tap controller and the Sight camera talking to the bar. Controller and camera traffic — no video ever rides them. Don't touch.
  • Is 1080p on an 86" TV going to look bad?
    It's what the room has always shown — the bar outputs 1080p60 max (Logitech's own spec) in every mode, even plugged straight into the TV. The fix changes where the picture goes, not what it looks like.
  • Do the TVs turn themselves on and off?
    Yes — Power save Mode 2 makes each TV sleep when the signal drops and wake the moment it returns. No remote hunting, no CEC dependence.

If It Fights You

symptom → move
  • Laptop test lights only the far TVThe near TV's old feed cable was borrowed to connect the bar. Restore that run (it exists — trace it), then continue. Still $0 parts.
  • Laptop doesn't take over / doesn't releaseDesk plate and bar are swapped at the Kramer. Desk = Input 1, bar = Input 2. Priority order is fixed in hardware — the fix is physical, not a setting.
  • TVs keep changing inputs on their ownAuto signal detection is still ON in the TV menu. Turn it off on both — the Kramer decides the source, not the TVs.
  • Meeting audio echoes or doublesThe green Phoenix plug is still on the Kramer feeding the old speaker DSP. Pull it — the bar's own speakers carry the meeting.
  • Screens black or scrambled after the room sleepsClassic EDID renegotiation. Flip Kramer DIP-3 (Lock EDID) ON. If a picture comes back at low res, power-cycle the TVs once after locking.
  • Bar says "no device connected" after wakingKnown CollabOS wake bug — restart the bar. If it recurs, adjust the room's power-saving policy in Teams admin rather than fighting it locally.
  • Far TV sparkles, drops out, or won't syncThe far wall is a long regular HDMI run (no Cat extender in this room). Reseat both ends; if it persists, swap in a high-speed / active HDMI cable rated for the length — or drop in an HDBaseT extender pair (see parts). At 1080p60 a good passive cable handles the distance.
