The job

Exit Bancroft Bar. 30-day landlord notice, auction the gear, park the Type 48 (4 CCR §65 / ABC-231) and sell the license within 12 months. Wind down the LLC + Inc out of court.

The debt that matters

Some of it is personal and survives closing the bar — payroll trust-fund tax, EDD, unpaid wages. That's the part to solve.

★ Why a tax lawyer leads

When the license sells, the escrow pays the IRS first, and ABC won't transfer it while taxes are owed — so the tax has to be handled before the sale closes. That's why a tax lawyer leads, not a bankruptcy lawyer.

On the first call

Questions to ask each one

  • The trust-fund designation (the big one). "When the Type 48 sells through the §24074 escrow and the IRS gets paid off the top, can we designate that payment to the trust-fund portion so it kills my personal TFRP — and when does that decision have to be locked in?" (This is the single highest-leverage move on your personal exposure. A pure wind-down lawyer won't raise it.)
  • Do you quarterback, or just your slice? "Can you run point and pull in the wage-defense and ABC/asset pieces and coordinate with my license lawyer — or do I coordinate that myself?"
  • Do you take a job this small? "I'm closing a single neighborhood bar — LLC owned by an S-corp, heavy debt across taxes, wages and trade creditors. Is this something you do for a business my size, or only bigger companies?"
  • Flat fee or hourly? "What's a realistic all-in, and what retainer do you need? I'm cash-poor right now — can it come out of the license-sale escrow?" (Caution: paying counsel ahead of the trust-fund taxes can look 'willful' under §6672 — let them structure it.)
  • Creditor sequencing. "I have present creditors today — how do we time the equipment auction and any payments so I don't create a fraudulent-transfer (UVTA §3439) or preference problem if someone later forces an involuntary bankruptcy?"
  • My personal hide. "Closing the entity doesn't erase what's on me — TFRP, EDD §1735, §558.1 wage liability, and a personal guarantee on the lease. Which of these can you actually move, and any way to cap the §203 waiting-time penalties?"
  • Homestead timing. "Should I record a declared homestead before any judgment lien hits — and is doing that now safe, not itself a fraudulent transfer?"
  • Coordinate + backstop. "I've got an old ABC/license lawyer I haven't used in ~10 years, and I've just made early contact on the ADA suit — can you take those over or coordinate? And if this falls apart, can you pivot me into Subchapter V or a last-resort personal Chapter 7?"
  • Logistics. "Is the first consult free, and what should I bring?"
