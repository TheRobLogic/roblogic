An Oral History The Lucero Brothers

An afternoon with Reinaldo “Pipo” and Valentín, recorded by Lisa — transcribed and translated two ways.

Reinaldo “Pipo” Lucero
Lisa’s Dad
Forty-five years installing floors. Crossed from Mexicali in January 1971 — one week before the Vietnam draft notice arrived. Married Nancy. Father of two.
Valentín “Vale” Lucero
Lisa’s Tío
Sold newspapers at six. ESL classes after emigrating, then high school in California. Welded warships, built Qualcomm high-rises, came back to the shipyard. Father of three.

Lisa sat down with her dad Reinaldo and her tío Valentín on an April afternoon, with a list of oral-history questions. They came from Mexicali. They emigrated as teenagers. The Vietnam draft notice arrived the week after they crossed. The recording runs nearly two hours. What follows is what they said — rendered two ways by two different machines, each with its own ear.
