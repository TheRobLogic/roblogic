How these picks are made

For each game, we went through every drawing that ever happened on a night like its next draw and counted how often each ball came up. How far back that goes depends on the game and its current ball matrix: 6/49 since 1988, BC/49 since 1992, Lotto Max since 2009, Daily Grand since 2016 — each card's own line below shows exactly how many draws and how far back its own picks are drawn from.

Rising = the balls that show up the most on these nights.

Retrograde = the ones that show up the least.

Aligned = a mix: the top two, the bottom two, and enough from the middle to fill out a complete ticket.

Daily Grand's Grand Number gets the same treatment in its own pool of 7. Lotto Max, 6/49, and BC/49 draw a bonus ball too, but it's machine-drawn — not something a ticket picks — so it's left out of these slips.

★ Logic’s Pick = This value ranking uses published fixed prizes and tonight's jackpots — not every prize tier. Lotto Max and Lotto 6/49 pay most of their smaller prizes as a share of that draw's ticket sales, which BCLC doesn't publish live, so those tiers aren't counted here. BC/49 and Daily Grand publish every tier as a fixed dollar amount, so their numbers are the fuller picture. Not a prediction — no combination is more likely to be drawn.
