Measured red flags

  • Scope mismatch: markets as solar, license is B-only. Solar PV under a bare B is permissible only when a project involves two or more unrelated trades; otherwise C-46 or C-10 is typically required. Worth asking what classification pulled the Salameh permit if it ever matters.
  • Qualifier / ownership handoff 11/18/2022: Wadhams out as RMG, Castaneda in as both RMO and 10%+ member on the same day. Common pattern but notable.
  • Workers'-comp exemption on a shop running 100+ permits/year — work is subcontracted or actual W-2 headcount is zero.
  • Marketing claim: "only company in the US installing graphene technology." Graphene PV is not a commercial product class. Puffery, not fraud.
  • Principal-disclosure inconsistencies across BBB (Cardoso / Guaderrama), CSLB (Wadhams then Castaneda), Alignable ("Tony Curtis"). Uneven rather than nefarious.
  • Volume taper: 270 permits (2023) → 77 (2024) → 35 (2025 partial). Consistent with post-NEM-3.0 market contraction.
  • Consumer-complaint surface (Yelp, Nextdoor, Angi): pushy door-to-door sales, one $1k-deposit-then-nothing (2022), a July 2025 complaint about Dec 2024 batteries still non-operational. No AG action, no class suit, no CSLB discipline.

The "misuse / impersonation" angle — not corroborated in public records

Zero news coverage, zero DA / AG press releases, zero CSLB enforcement bulletins, zero court filings where Kermit Wadhams appears as victim of license fraud or identity theft. One "Desmit vs. Wadhams" SDSC civil case (2020) names a different person (Kevin L. Wadhams) and is not relevant.

What does show up is structural rather than victimization: Wadhams is a classic qualifier-for-hire — a long-licensed contractor whose name backed an LLC run by non-licensees (in this case Cardoso / Guaderrama at Cyberdyne from 02/25/2019 to 11/18/2022). CSLB has been cracking down on this practice under B&P Code §7068.1. If that is the misuse pattern Rob heard about, it is voluntary on Wadhams' part, not theft of his identity. Separate category.
