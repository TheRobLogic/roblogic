Dear Bill and Lana,

Congrats on wrapping up the build. Few things about the shared property line I figured were worth putting on paper. Easier than trying to catch you in the driveway, and gives you a chance to look each one over on your own time.

1. Water at the property line.

After the storm on [DATE] I took water in the basement and a mature orange tree came down in saturated soil along the fence. Want to understand what the finished grade looks like from your side, and whether water's running toward my lot now in a way it didn't used to. Want to walk the line with you sometime, especially behind the patio, and sort it out. More rain coming, I'd rather get ahead of it.

2. Fill and fence.

Some of the fill on your side has migrated under the slab edge onto my patio, and in one stretch the fence has been pushed to the ground. Easy stuff to miss at the tail end of a big project. Want to walk this with you too and figure out a fix on the retaining and fence side. Doesn't need to be a production.

3. Exterior lights.

A few of the fixtures on your building shine into my house and yard and stay on all night. Probably just needs some shielding or re-aiming so the light stays on your side. Small fix, but a real difference for me.

4. Something after the rain.

One more worth you checking on your end. After one of the storm events I saw something that looked and smelled like sanitary effluent coming from your side of the line onto mine. Not claiming to know where it's coming from. Could you have your plumber take a look at your lateral and let me know what they find? If it's coming from somewhere else entirely I'd rather we figure that out together than guess.

Writing this hoping we can sort it out between us. I'd rather a conversation now than either of us having to involve anyone else later. Give me a call in the next couple of weeks and we can walk the line. [PHONE].

Appreciate it.
