Re: Formal complaint & request for enforcement action regarding unpermitted grading, stormwater & sewage discharge, and light trespass at 4862 Palm Avenue (APN 4942510500) and 4870 Palm Avenue (APN 4942510400), La Mesa, CA 91941

Dear Building Official and Code Compliance Supervisor:

I am the owner of record of 4852 Palm Avenue (APN 4942510700) and 4854 Palm Avenue (APN 4942510600), which adjoin the subject properties to the south. I am writing to report multiple continuing violations of the La Mesa Municipal Code and the California Building Code at the neighboring parcels at 4862 Palm Avenue (APN 4942510500) and 4870 Palm Avenue (APN 4942510400), both owned of record by Waleed A. Salameh since July 23, 1993. The construction at 4862 is, to my understanding, now complete, yet the as-built site conditions — unpermitted fill, the absence of any engineered retaining wall at the shared line, the resulting uncontrolled runoff and sewage discharge onto my property, and the resulting damage to the shared fence and to my lateral support — persist and continue to damage my property. The sewage discharge also presents an immediate public-health concern. I respectfully request that the City investigate, issue appropriate enforcement action, and compel the owner to remediate each condition retroactively as set out in Section III below.

This letter should be read in conjunction with the 19-page permit-history record produced by the City in response to my California Public Records Act request PRA #26-237 (filed 4/14/2026, closed 4/16/2026), which I rely on for the permit numbers cited below. A further, narrower CPRA request (addressed to the City Clerk) follows separately.

I. Violations complained of

  1. Unpermitted grading and fill; loss of lateral support.
    The applicant has raised finished grade on the 4862 side of our shared property line by approximately one to two feet (exact depth to be confirmed by survey) with no engineered retaining wall at the property line. Fill material is migrating from beneath the edge of the applicant's concrete slab onto my parcel, and lateral pressure has physically deformed — in one location crushed flat — the shared boundary fence. See Exhibits 4, 5, 6, and 9B (GPS-verified at the shared line): Exhibit 4 (2024-10-13) shows the construction state with no retaining wall at the slab edge and a fully collapsed fence section; Exhibit 5 — a key photograph — shows the grade differential between Salameh's raised fill and my concrete patio, with only a makeshift plywood panel standing in for a retaining wall; Exhibit 6 shows dirt migrating from beneath Salameh's slab edge onto my patio surface; and Exhibit 9B (2024-09-27, Live Photo keyframe) is a low-angle cross-section showing the chain-link fence pinned flat by the weight of the fill with loose soil spilling directly onto my side. Exhibit 9 (2024-09-27) further shows that the condition pre-dates the October 2024 documentation by over two weeks.
    • The grading has altered the natural drainage course (see §II below), which removes it from the "no permit" exemption of CBC Appendix J, §J103.2(8), as adopted by LMMC §14.05.020, regardless of depth or volume.
    • LMMC §24.05.030(K) requires residential grading to be "minimal" and to "leave natural appearing landforms."
    • LMMC §24.05.030(I)(6) limits any retaining wall in a required side-yard setback to the height needed to retain a three-foot fill; no such wall appears to exist.
    • Cal. Civ. Code §832 guarantees me the lateral and subjacent support that my land receives from the adjoining lot. The migrating fill and fence deflection are direct evidence of a breach of that duty.
    • The shared fence is subject to Cal. Civ. Code §841. See Exhibits 1, 2, 3, 7, and 8: fence damage, displaced top rail, construction lumber pressed against the fence from Salameh's side, and fresh-cut wood with sawdust at fence-post locations.
  2. Stormwater runoff onto my property; flooding and vegetation loss.
    The regraded 4862 lot is now graded toward my property, with a high proportion of impervious surface and no on-site retention or approved drainage conveyance. During the storm event on [DATE OF STORM], runoff from 4862 entered my lot, flooded my basement, and saturated the ground to the point that an established orange tree fell. Similar, smaller runoff events have occurred during [LIST OTHER DATES IF KNOWN].
    • LMMC §7.18.040(A)–(B): the discharge of sediment and construction-related waste into the City's MS4 is an "illegal discharge." §7.18.140(A): eroded soils on disturbed slopes must be secured before the rainy season. §7.18.220: "any violation of this chapter is a public nuisance." Penalties are set by §7.18.230.
    • Cal. Civ. Code §§3479–3481: private and public nuisance. §§3501–3503: remedies.
    • Keys v. Romley, 64 Cal.2d 396 (1966); Locklin v. City of Lafayette, 7 Cal.4th 327 (1994); Sheffet v. County of Los Angeles, 3 Cal.App.3d 720 (1970): an upper landowner who alters natural drainage and causes unreasonable injury to the lower landowner is liable under the "reasonable use" rule.
    • Cal. Water Code §13304(a): regulatory cleanup liability for discharges to waters of the state.
  3. Sewage discharge onto my property.
    On [DATE OF SEWAGE INCIDENT], a sewage pump on the 4862 side failed and discharged sewage onto my parcel. The condition has not been demonstrated to be permanently corrected.
    • LMMC §17.08.030 authorizes the City Engineer to inspect any lateral discharging to the public sewer and to issue a corrective directive where the improper use or maintenance of a lateral "causes or contributes to the discharge of septic wastewater… or any other objectionable substance."
    • LMMC §7.18.040(B)(1) identifies sewage as a prohibited non-stormwater discharge.
    • Cal. Health & Safety Code §5411 prohibits discharging sewage "in any manner which will result in contamination, pollution or a nuisance."
    • Cal. Water Code §13304(a): cleanup liability.
    I am contemporaneously reporting this condition to the San Diego County Department of Environmental Health & Quality (1-888-846-0800) and to the San Diego Regional Water Quality Control Board (Region 9) via the CalEPA Environmental Complaint System.
  4. Light trespass from unshielded fixtures.
    Multiple unshielded exterior lights installed on the 4862 structure cast direct, high-intensity glare onto my dwelling and yard and, on information and belief, operate continuously through the overnight hours. This is a textbook violation of the only on-point La Mesa standard:
    • LMMC §24.05.020(D)(16): "All lighting shall be designed, installed and maintained to project the light primarily on the owner's property… Any light falling on adjacent properties shall be minimal and incidental. Lighting shall be focused directly on the owner's property, and shall not be focused on adjacent properties."
    • Also Cal. Civ. Code §3479 (nuisance — anything "offensive to the senses" that interferes with the comfortable enjoyment of property).

II. Related permit-record anomalies

The record produced in PRA #26-237 and corroborated on the City's public permit portal raises additional questions that bear directly on the scope of the violations above:

  • Building permit B21-885 (the parent permit for the current structure on 4862) is not publicly searchable; only its revision, B21-885-REV-01, is, and it shows "Finaled" on 1/5/2026 at situs "4860 Palm Ave." I request confirmation that the as-built improvements match the approved scope.
  • Plan-change permit PORT2024-0816 — describing second- and third-floor balconies, balcony roof covers, new stairs/footings, new entry doors, and a trash enclosure — is shown as VOIDED. If any of the work described in that voided application was nonetheless performed, it is unpermitted.
  • Permits B21-825, B21-826, and B21-827 (three 2021 applications, each at approximately $157,000 valuation) have remained in "Applied" status for roughly five years without issuance or withdrawal. I ask the Building Division to clarify their current disposition.
  • Two BLDG-FINAL inspections on solar PV permit 2023-1411 were CANCELLED on 7/23/2024 and 7/24/2024. I request that the Building Division explain the reason for those cancellations.

III. Requested actions

I respectfully request that the City:

  1. Conduct an on-site inspection of 4862 and 4870 Palm Avenue to document current grade elevations, drainage pattern, retaining-wall condition, sewer-lateral condition, and exterior-lighting configuration;
  2. Compare the as-built structure and site work at 4862 against the approved scope of B21-885 (and REV-01) and determine whether any work described in voided permit PORT2024-0816 was in fact performed as part of the now-completed construction;
  3. Review the validity of the B21-885-REV-01 "Finaled" status dated 1/5/2026 in light of the site conditions documented in Exhibits 1–9B, and determine whether the Certificate of Occupancy (if any) was properly issued notwithstanding those conditions — and, if not, revoke or suspend it until the conditions are remediated;
  4. Issue a Notice of Violation and initiate administrative proceedings under LMMC Ch. 10.150 (nuisance abatement) and Ch. 10.170 (land-use violations), with the authority to assess civil penalties, to record a cost-recovery lien against the property, and to impose joint and several personal liability on the owner under §10.170.030(b)(5)(a). Because construction is complete, enforcement should proceed directly to Notice of Violation and administrative abatement rather than stop-work, but each day that the listed conditions persist is a continuing violation under Ch. 10.150 / Ch. 10.170 and Ch. 7.18;
  5. Require, as retroactive conditions of compliance, that the owner: (a) design, permit, and construct an engineered retaining wall at the shared property line sufficient to contain the existing fill, per LMMC §24.05.030(I)(6) and CBC §105.2 exception 3; (b) design, permit, and construct site drainage that returns pre-development runoff conditions or better, subject to Public Works sign-off, to mitigate the discharge documented in Grievance II; (c) repair, pressure-test, and certify the sewer lateral and pump serving 4862 per LMMC §17.08.030; (d) replace all non-conforming exterior lighting with shielded, downward-directed fixtures compliant with §24.05.020(D)(16); and (e) repair or replace the shared boundary fence, with cost allocation as prescribed by Cal. Civ. Code §841;
  6. Condition any future permit, plan revision, or further occupancy sign-off on these parcels upon full completion of the above remediation;
  7. Refer the sewage-discharge condition to the City Engineer under §17.08.030 and to the San Diego County Department of Environmental Health & Quality.
