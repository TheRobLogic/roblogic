MATH day

Lane: SURFER

The Surfer

Momentum: buys names up >=2% from open on >=1.5x average volume; targets +2%, stops -1%; up to 3 picks/day.

MATH day

Lane: BARGAIN

The Bargain Hunter

Mean reversion: buys names down >=2% from open that have NO same-day news signal (no falling knife with a known reason); targets +1.5%, stops -1%; up to 3 picks/day. rsi_max is NOT evaluated -- a real RSI needs a day-by-day historical close series the packet doesn't carry (only 20d aggregates); see engine/league_fighters.py's module docstring.

MATH day

Lane: NEWSBUG

The News Junkie

Buys symbols with a same-morning news_signals conviction mapped to a score >=0.7 (news_mech.py has no literal 0-1 score field -- high/medium/low conviction is mapped onto one) and a bullish keyword match against the row's own headline/theme/suggestion text, confirmed by positive momentum from open; targets +2.5%, stops -1.25%; up to 3 picks/day. keyword_match is always-on regardless of this value -- there is no code path that reads it to disable the filter.

MATH day

Lane: CHARTBOT

Chart Robot

Price-only technical: buys a break above the prior day's high confirmed by >=1.2x average volume, no news/fundamentals input at all; targets +2%, stops -1%; up to 3 picks/day.

MATH day

Lane: GAPPER

The Gap Player

Trades symbols gapping >=3% at the open, following the gap direction (continuation, not fade) -- gap-UP only, Season 1 is long-only end to end; targets +2.5%, stops -1.25%; up to 3 picks/day. fade_or_follow is hardcoded to follow in code, not read as a live toggle -- flipping this value would not change behavior.

MATH day

Lane: SHADOW

Insider Shadow

Form 4 tape: buys symbols with a high-conviction insider BUY (>=$100k) filed within the last 5 days (integrations.edgar); targets +3%, stops -1.5%; up to 2 picks/day.

MATH multiday

Lane: DRIFTER

Earnings Drifter

PEAD (post-earnings-announcement drift): buys symbols that beat with a >=5% surprise and holds up to 5 trading days (multiday division, not flattened at close); targets +5%, stops -2.5%; up to 2 picks/day.

MATH overnight

Lane: SLEEPER

The Sleeper

Overnight division: buys at yesterday's close targeting the next day's open, ranking the packet's own candidate shortlist by 20-day return (return_pct_20d) -- the closest real multi-day number the packet carries, since a rolling close-to-open edge needs a day-by-day close/open history the packet doesn't have; falls back to a single SPY pick if no candidate has usable data. Up to 3 picks/day. No stop or target: the referee's overnight division exits by selling at next day's open only, never consulting stop_px/target_px -- lookback_days/target_pct/stop_pct are registered but not evaluated.

The Rules

Twelve lanes trade the exact same frozen morning packet every trading day — same prices, same news, same insider tape — so no lane gets an information edge over another. These terms were hashed and locked before the first pick was ever emitted; changing any of them mid-season means starting the clock over.

TermRule
Bankroll$10,000 per fighter, compounding
Position limitsup to 4 open at once, none larger than 25% of bankroll
UniverseS&P 500 allowlist only
Decision time15 minutes after the opening bell
Day lanesflat by the closing bell every day
Overnight / multiday lanesmay carry a position past the close (see each fighter’s division)
Trading costs7.5 basis points per side, charged on every fill
Same-bar rulestop and target both fill on the same bar → the stop wins (conservative scoring, never the lucky read)
Season length8 weeks from the opening bell

The Two Dummies

Dummy: SPY Buy-Hold buys SPY at open+15m and sells flat at the close, every day — no thinking involved.

Dummy: Coin-Flip draws one S&P 500 symbol at random each day from a date-seeded generator (replayable — the same date always draws the same symbol).

Neither dummy claims any edge. They’re the floor every real lane has to clear.

The Winner Bar

Season 1’s winner is whoever ends with the highest bankroll and beats both dummies by at least 2 percentage points of total return. If nobody clears that bar, the verdict is “day-trade lane fails” — no lane gets crowned just for finishing first. No mid-season tweaks: any rule change resets the clock and starts a new season.
