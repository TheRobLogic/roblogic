Discovery questions

Rough answers are fine. We'll fill in the rest on the call.

  1. How many people are in the practice, and how many of them would use the shared drive?
  2. One office, or more than one? Does anyone work from home or see clients by video?
  3. What would go on the drive: client files and session notes, or office stuff like forms, billing and HR? Both?
  4. Do you already use a notes or scheduling system like SimplePractice or TherapyNotes? What lives in there?
  5. Do you bill insurance? Electronically?
  6. What do you use for email and calendar today: regular Gmail, Google Workspace, Microsoft 365, something else?
  7. What computers do people use? Practice-owned or personal, Mac or Windows?
  8. Where do documents live right now, and how do you share them with each other?
  9. Who set up your internet and Wi-Fi, and who's the provider? Is there a printer already, and does it do Wi-Fi?
  10. Has anyone done a HIPAA risk assessment or written security policies for the practice? Does your malpractice or business insurance ask for anything on that?
  11. After it's set up, do you want someone on call and managing it month to month, or a one-time setup you run yourselves?
  12. Any timeline, and is there something that prompted this now (a move, a new hire, insurance, something that went wrong)?
