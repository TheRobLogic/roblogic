02

The Pitch & The Lane

Every cold email, every one-sheet, every pitch goes out with the same positioning. Internal consistency is what gets you taken seriously — bookers and music directors talk to each other, and they smell a band that hasn't decided what it is.

Official One-Sheet Language

Homeless Sexuals are a San Diego noise-rock / post-punk band in the Flipper / Scratch Acid / Jesus Lizard / Butthole Surfers lineage. Confrontational vocals over a three-guitar wall and live-tracked rhythm section. For fans of METZ, Pissed Jeans, Chat Pile, Bambara, Uniform, A Place To Bury Strangers, Pile.

That comp list is the most important asset in this brief. Those are the bands a music director or talent buyer knows by name and knows what to do with on a bill. They are not peers — they're one tier up. That's the point. We're pitching into a lane that already has flow, not asking gatekeepers to invent a category.

What we are not: indie rock, garage revival, egg-punk, fuzz-pop, or anything with a Wavves comparison. The first PR agent who tries to put a Mac DeMarco comp in front of a booker will tank the pitch. Hold the line on the lineage.

03

The One Hurdle

We're Bandcamp-only. No Spotify, no Apple Music, no Tidal, no YouTube Music. Three independent checks confirmed it — the search engines have nothing, and an Apple Music direct search returns an explicit "No results."

The Honest Read

The DSPs are a scam. Everyone in this lineup of bands knows it. The pennies-per-stream economics are insulting, the Spotify-as-podcast-company pivot is gross, and the indie-friendly half of the industry has been quietly bailing on them all year. We're not pretending otherwise.

But: for the people we're pitching, DSP presence is the difference between "real release worth listening to" and "Bandcamp link, skip." That's the read in 2026, fair or not. It's a hurdle, not a showstopper.

Where it matters:

  • PR firms — they pitch into Spotify algorithmic playlisting, Apple Music for Artists, editorial pitches. No DSP = no campaign.
  • Some commercial radio + KCRW's algorithmic shoulder programming.
  • Discovery surfaces — "Fans of METZ also listen to…" is how listeners who'd love us find us.

Where it doesn't matter:

  • KEXP's submission guidelines accept Bandcamp streaming + WAV download — and as the industry bails on Spotify, public radio's posture has loosened. Not a hard gate.
  • Post-Trash, Razorcake, Maximum Rocknroll, college radio, WFMU, Bandcamp Daily (literally owned by Bandcamp) — Bandcamp links are the standard.
  • SD/LA bookers — none of them care, they just want to hear the songs.
Recommended Move

DistroKid, ~$23/year, unlimited uploads. Pick three or four tracks that are studio-mixed or close to it (or finish-mixing two of the demos). Push to Spotify + Apple + Tidal + YouTube Music. Total time to live: roughly five days. Have it up before the Casbah show. Then the rest of this brief unlocks.

When We're Ready to Scale

Hiring out PR and a booking agent is the move once we have a finished record, a verified DSP presence, and at least one of the Phase 02 wins on the books (Post-Trash review, Bandcamp Daily feature, KEXP rotation, or a Treefort slot). Trying to skip ahead here wastes money — agents don't make magic, they amplify existing momentum.

Realistic budget for a credible 3-month album campaign: $4,500–12,000 total (PR at $1,500–4,000/mo, booking agent on commission).
