Booking agent

  • Panache — Michelle Cable — LA
    michelle@panacherock.com · (415) 246-5405
    Roster: Mac DeMarco, King Gizzard, Ty Segall, SPELLLING, Murlocs. Only major agent who openly courts DIY without a label attached. Approach after we land a PR firm or a press win — not cold.

One thing that's blocking the radio + PR list: we're Bandcamp-only — no Spotify, no Apple Music, no Tidal. The DSPs are a scam and we all know it, but in 2026 they're the necessary evil. PR firms literally can't run a campaign without DSP discovery surfaces, and KEXP's MD treats DSP presence as a "real release" signal even though their guidelines accept Bandcamp + WAV.

DistroKid, ~$23/yr, unlimited uploads. Three studio-mixed tracks live on Spotify + Apple + Tidal + YouTube Music in about five days. After that the whole list above unlocks. Bandcamp Daily, Post-Trash, Razorcake, MRR, college radio, and the festivals don't need it.
